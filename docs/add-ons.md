# Add-ons

[Back to the README](../README.md) | [Русский](ru/add-ons.md)

The **Add-ons** tab holds optional extras. Each one opens in its own window and does nothing while it is switched off. Once switched on it keeps working in the background, so its window does not need to stay open.

## Tactics Board

Plan rounds on the real maps, the way a team plans for a tournament.

- **Draw the plan.** Place units, draw routes, arrows and areas, and add notes. Routes are timed paths: every unit moves along its own path, with waits where it holds a position.
- **Play it back.** Strategies are split into phases on a timeline, so you can scrub through the round and watch every unit move in step.
- **Every floor.** Maps with several levels, such as bunkers, have each floor on its own layer (PageUp / PageDown steps through them).
- **Teams and rosters.** Assign players to the five slots, and reuse a strategy on another roster without redrawing it.
- **Library and sharing.** Keep your strategies in a library, share them as a file, a code or a link, show them in presentation mode, and export an image.

Map packs are installed per map from the maps screen, or all at once. The same packs draw the real map under your in-game radar (Game UI > Minimap). The Tactics Board needs your game account, which Sentinel reads from the running game.

## Replays (beta)

- **A library of your replays.** The game keeps only your ten most recent replays and deletes the oldest. Save the ones worth keeping and the game can no longer delete them. Search by map, mode, tank or agent.
- **Play anything.** Play a replay with one click, open one from any folder, drop it on the window, or let Sentinel take over `.replay` files so a double-click plays them. Files from other games are refused.
- **Free camera.** Fly through the battle, or look through the gun of the tank you follow. Orbit a point, fly level, outline every tank through walls, draw where every gun is pointing and the path of every shot, and open a panel with the followed tank's health, energy, abilities, equipment, perks and modules.
- **Rewind.** Jump back and the battle is as it was then: destroyed tanks are back and the score rolls back. Pause freezes the battle while the game's interface keeps working.
- **Sound and keys.** You hear the tank you follow. Every key can be rebound, and mouse feel, fly speed, smoothing and field of view are adjustable.

Battles played in the replay player are never recorded as real battles.

## HEAT Snap

When you destroy a streamer who is live in HEAT, or one destroys you, HEAT Snap clips that moment from their Twitch broadcast.

- Connect your Twitch account once. Clips are made on your account and also appear in your Twitch clip manager.
- Streamers are recognised from a public list curated by the developer, tied to in-game accounts where known, and by a live HEAT stream on a channel matching a player's nickname.
- Clip delay and length are adjustable, and a desktop notification can tell you when a clip is ready.
- Streamers on your team show up with their live status before the fight. Enemy streamers are never listed, but a clip is still made when you meet one.

Streamed HEAT and want your entry off the list? Message the developer on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/AjfcuhDDw5).

## HEAT Sabotage

For streamers: your viewers spend channel points to switch off parts of your HUD for a while.

- Ready-made effects hide the crosshair and reload displays, your status panel, the markers above vehicles, the objectives, the scoreboard or the minimap, or everything at once.
- Build your own rewards from the same element list as Game UI > Battle HUD.
- Set the price, duration and cooldown of each effect, and how much time one effect can bank when it is bought again while running.
- Rewards are on sale only during a battle. A redemption that lands outside one is refunded. Optional chat lines announce when an effect starts, ends or is refunded.

Channel points need a Twitch Affiliate or Partner channel. HEAT Sabotage only changes the rewards it creates.
