Rules

Mark thresholds (index x100)

Progress scale

Linear: 0% at average 0 and 65 / 85 / 95% at marks 1 / 2 / 3, so every battle moves progress in proportion to its own index, from a tank's first battle. Percentile: where the average sits among regular players, which reads 0% for a new tank's first battles. Neither changes marks.

Try your own balance

Every number above and every factor below becomes editable, and the tables show what your change does to each tank against the published pack. It stays in this browser.

This changes nothing for anyone. A pack you build here is a proposal and nothing more: it is never uploaded, and no install will ever fetch it. If you think it is right, send the downloaded JSON and the changelog to the author, who is the only one who publishes calibration.

Tanks

Average DPM needed for
A tank's factor is what an average battle in it is worth: above 1.0 the tank is expected to do more damage, so a mark costs more. Lobby: the factor measured on other players in the research lobbies, before shrinking toward 1.0, with its result count. Editing: wheel over a factor steps 0.001, Ctrl+wheel 0.01, Alt+wheel 0.1. Click a heading to sort.
